The Atelier

Where steel becomes memory

Each CHRONOS passes through 142 hands and 1,200 hours before it earns its serial number.

01

Hand-finishing

Bezels are satin-brushed and polished by hand at alternating angles to catch light at every turn.

02

Movement assembly

Master watchmakers assemble calibers under microscopes, regulating each to ±2 seconds per day.

03

Pressure testing

Lifetime servicing
